# P1 — Why does PF++ form a coherent Juglet shape when GARF and TORA do not?

**Status:** largely answered, 2026-07-20 · **Blocked by:** none

## The answer, as it stands

The decision matrix's **middle row**: the learned prior **composes**, and the tokens
**gate plausibility**. Wear-invariance was confirmed at both the output and the feature
level. Executed as T0–T4 on 2026-07-20.

An earlier and equally important part of the answer: **much of the apparent gap was
inference and visualisation, not understanding.** GARF and TORA were not failing as badly
as their pictures suggested — TORA's scattered-looking output is the correct paper-style
proposal, and it looks scattered because the flow target is the scan's table layout rather
than a broken-object layout.

That is failure mode 2 — **the measurement was broken** — dressed up as failure mode 1.
It is worth stating in the thesis in those terms.

## Why this stays open rather than closed

Closing it would license the sentence "PF++ generalises to archaeological pottery", which
the evidence does not support. The mechanism is understood; the **scope** of the claim is
not. Keep the number, keep it open, and do not reuse it.
